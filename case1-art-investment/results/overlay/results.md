**Effect of a 4% art sleeve:** return −0.09 pp/yr, vol −0.19 pp, max drawdown +0.1 pp (i.e., no change).
Crisis drawdowns: 2022 inflation shock 60/40 −16.5% vs +4% art −16.4% (no protection).

**Read:** at a realistic satellite weight, art is a do-nothing allocation — the theoretical −4%
variance reduction (M1) shows up as ~0.2 pp of vol and zero drawdown protection, before art's
illiquidity, transaction costs (~25% round-trip auction), storage, and insurance — none of which
are in these numbers and all of which make the real-world result worse.
